How it works

Three steps to every verse

Read the numbers

Each number is one Arabic letter. The same number is always the same letter in that verse.

Find the letters

Guess with the alphabetical keyboard. Each right letter fills in across the whole verse.

Reveal the ayah

The full verse appears in mushaf script, with your stars, score and streak.

Questions

Good to know

Is the Quran text accurate?

Yes. The verses come from Tanzil, a widely used verified source, and reviewed by people who know the Quran well. The text is always Arabic and never altered; only the letters are hidden for the game.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free with ads between puzzles. Ads are never shown on a verse. A single "Remove ads" purchase turns them off forever.

Do I need to read Arabic well?

Knowing the Arabic letters is enough. Start on Gentle, where many letters are already shown, and use hints when you are stuck.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All verses are inside the app. Only ads need a connection.

Do you collect my data?

There is no account. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are served by Google AdMob, as explained in our privacy policy.
