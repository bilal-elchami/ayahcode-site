رمز الآية

A calm puzzle game for every age. Each letter of a Quran verse is hidden behind a number. Find the letters, and the ayah reveals itself, in Arabic, exactly as revealed.

Coming soon on Google Play and the App Store

Decode, letter by letter

Every number stands for one Arabic letter. Each letter you find fills in across the whole verse.

Made for every reader

Large letters, simple controls and gentle hints, so grandparents and grandchildren can play alike.

A journey through the Quran

Play verse after verse, keep a daily streak with the Ayah of the day, and work toward completing the whole Quran.
